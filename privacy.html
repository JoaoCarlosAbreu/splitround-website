SplitRound — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 August 2026

SplitRound is a mobile app for splitting shared expenses between friends, housemates and travel groups. This policy explains what data the app collects, why, who it is shared with, and what control you have over it.

The data controller is João Abreu, Portugal. For any privacy question or request, contact joaoabreu.wd@gmail.com.

What we collect

DataWhy
NameTo identify you to other members of your groups.
Email address and/or phone numberTo sign you in, send account emails, and let people you know invite you to a group. At least one is required.
PasswordStored only as a cryptographic hash. We never store or can read your password.
Google account identifierOnly if you choose "Sign in with Google". We receive your Google ID and verified email; we never receive your Google password.
Expenses, groups, payments, comments and balancesThe core function of the app — the shared ledger you and your group create.
Receipt photos and group iconsOnly images you choose to attach. See "Images" below.
Device push tokensTo deliver notifications. Removed when you disable notifications.
Notification and visibility preferencesTo respect your choices, including whether others may find you by email or phone.

What we do not do

  • No advertising, and no ad identifiers.
  • No analytics or tracking SDKs of any kind — the app contains none.
  • No selling or renting of personal data, ever.
  • No access to your contacts, location, or files beyond images you explicitly pick.
  • No bank account or card connection. SplitRound records who owes what; it never moves money.

Images, and an important limitation

Receipt photos and group icons are stored with our image host (Cloudinary) and delivered over HTTPS from URLs that are not individually access-controlled. Anyone who obtains an image's URL can view that image without signing in.

Please bear this in mind before attaching a receipt. Receipts can show names, addresses, and partial card numbers. Only attach images you would be comfortable being viewable by anyone holding the link.

Who your data is shared with

Data you enter into a group is visible to the other members of that group — that is the purpose of a shared ledger. Beyond that, we use these processors to run the service:

ServicePurpose
RenderApplication hosting.
MongoDBDatabase storage.
CloudinaryStorage and delivery of receipt and group images.
ResendSending account emails (invitations, password resets).
ExpoDelivering push notifications to your device.
GoogleVerifying your identity, only if you use "Sign in with Google".

Currency exchange rates are fetched from the public Frankfurter API. No personal data is sent in those requests.

These providers may process data outside your country, including outside the European Economic Area. We rely on the safeguards offered by each provider for such transfers.

Stored on your device

The app keeps your sign-in token, a cached copy of your groups and expenses for offline use, and your theme, language and app-lock preferences on the device itself. If you enable the app lock, biometric and passcode verification is handled entirely by your operating system — SplitRound never receives your fingerprint, face data or passcode. Uninstalling the app removes all of this local data.

Your rights and choices

You can, from within the app: change your name, email and phone; change your password; turn each category of notification on or off; and stop others from finding you by email or phone.

You may also delete your account from within the app, under Account → Close account. That erases your personal data — name, email, phone number, password, Google link, devices and friendships — immediately and permanently. Expenses and payments you shared with others remain visible to those group members, because a shared ledger cannot be made accurate for some participants and erased for others; every copy of your name in them is replaced with the neutral label “Former member”. The account deletion page sets out exactly what is deleted, what is kept and for how long, and how to request deletion by email instead.

If you want a copy of your data, email joaoabreu.wd@gmail.com and we will act on your request within 30 days.

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, you have the right to access, correct, erase, restrict, object to the processing of, and receive a portable copy of your personal data, and to complain to your national data protection authority. We process your data to perform our contract with you (running the service), and on the basis of legitimate interests for security and abuse prevention.

Retention

We keep your account data for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, the personal data listed above is erased at once; the shared expense records that other members' balances depend on are kept, anonymised, for as long as those groups exist. Residual copies may remain in routine database backups until those backups are rotated out, within 30 days.

Children

SplitRound is not directed at children and is not intended for use by anyone under 16. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Security

All traffic between the app and our servers is encrypted with HTTPS. Passwords are stored only as hashes. No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security — please use a strong, unique password.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy materially we will update the date at the top and, where appropriate, notify you in the app.
