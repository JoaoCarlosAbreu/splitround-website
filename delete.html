SplitRound — Delete your account

Last updated: 15 August 2026

This page explains how to delete your SplitRound account (developer: João Abreu, Portugal) and exactly what happens to your data when you do. You do not need the app installed to make the request.

Option 1 — From inside the app

  1. Open SplitRound and sign in.
  2. Go to the Account tab.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Close account.
  4. Confirm. Your account and personal data are deleted immediately and you are signed out.

Option 2 — By email

Email joaoabreu.wd@gmail.com with the subject “SplitRound account deletion”, from the email address registered on the account. Include the name on the account so it can be identified. Requests are acted on within 30 days, and you receive confirmation once the account is deleted.

Deleting is permanent. There is no undo and no recovery period. If you sign up again afterwards you start with a new, empty account.

What is deleted

DataWhen
Your nameImmediately
Your email address and phone numberImmediately
Your password (its stored hash) and any password-reset tokensImmediately
The link to your Google account, if you used “Sign in with Google”Immediately
Your device push tokens and notification preferencesImmediately
Your friend list, and your entry in other people's friend listsImmediately
Everything the app stored on your deviceWhen you uninstall the app

What is kept, and why

The expenses, payments and comments you shared with a group are not yours alone — they are what every other member's balance is calculated from. Erasing them would silently change what other people owe each other, so those records stay in the groups they belong to. They no longer carry your name: every copy of it is replaced with the neutral label “Former member”, and what is left pointing at the deleted account is an internal identifier with nothing attached to it.

KeptFor how long
Expense and settlement rows you were part of, with your name replaced by “Former member”As long as the group exists
Comments you wrote, and mentions of you, with your name replaced by “Former member”As long as the group exists
Activity-feed entries in your former groups, with your name replaced by “Former member”As long as the group exists
Receipt photos you attached to a shared expenseAs long as that expense exists
Residual copies in routine database backupsUntil the backup is rotated out, up to 30 days

If you want a receipt photo you uploaded removed as well, delete it from the expense before closing your account, or say so in your email request.

Deleting a single expense or group instead

You do not have to delete your account to remove specific data. Inside the app you can delete an expense, delete or archive a group, remove a receipt photo, and change your name, email or phone at any time.
